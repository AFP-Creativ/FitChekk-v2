**For**: All Team Members, Leadership  
**Read Time**: 8 minutes  
**Last Updated**: November 2025

## Team Overview

**Size**: 5-7 people (Months 1-12)  
**Structure**: Flat, collaborative, cross-functional  
**Methodology**: Agile (2-week sprints)  
**Tools**: Slack (communication), GitHub (code), Notion (project management), Figma (design)

---

## Organizational Chart

```
CEO / Product Lead
    ├── Engineering (2-3 people)
    │   ├── iOS Lead Engineer (TCA, SwiftUI)
    │   ├── iOS Engineer (Features, AI integration)
    │   └── Backend Engineer (Supabase, AI APIs)
    │
```

## Roles & Responsibilities

### CEO / Product Lead

**Primary Responsibilities**:
- Product vision and strategy
- Fundraising and investor relations
- Team hiring and management
- Key partnerships and business development
- Final decision maker on product direction

**Day-to-Day**:
- Define and prioritize product roadmap
- Write user stories and requirements
- Review designs and features
- Sprint planning and backlog grooming
- User research and feedback analysis
- Monitor key metrics (KPIs)

**Success Metrics**:
- Product-market fit achieved (NPS > 50)
- Fundraising goals met ($800K seed)
- Team is productive and happy (low turnover)
- Business metrics hit targets (users, revenue)

**Skills Required**:
- Product management (5+ years)
- Strategic thinking
- Stakeholder management
- User empathy
- Data-driven decision making

**Reports To**: Board / Investors  
**Direct Reports**: All team members (small team)

## Collaboration Model

### Daily Standup (15 minutes, 9:00 AM PT)

**Format**: Async-first (Slack thread), Sync if needed

**Each person shares**:
1. **Yesterday**: What I accomplished
2. **Today**: What I'm working on
3. **Blockers**: Any impediments

**Example**:
```
Emma (iOS Engineer):
✅ Yesterday: Completed WardrobeFeature reducer, tests passing
🚧 Today: Working on ItemDetailView, will pair with Design
❌ Blockers: Waiting on API endpoint for image upload

Marcus (Designer):
✅ Yesterday: Finished HomeView mockups
🚧 Today: Starting OutfitCreationView design
❌ Blockers: None
```

---

### Sprint Planning (Monday, 10:00 AM, 1 hour)

**Attendees**: Everyone

**Agenda**:
1. Review last sprint (5 min)
2. Demo completed features (15 min)
3. Retrospective (15 min)
4. Plan next sprint (25 min)
   - Review backlog
   - Estimate stories (story points)
   - Commit to sprint goals

**Output**: Sprint backlog with assigned tickets

---

### Weekly Design Review (Wednesday, 2:00 PM, 30 min)

**Attendees**: Designer, iOS Lead, CEO

**Agenda**:
1. Review designs for upcoming features
2. Discuss implementation feasibility
3. Iterate based on feedback
4. Approve for development

---

### Code Review Process

**All code must be reviewed before merging.**

**Process**:
1. Engineer creates PR with clear description
2. Assign reviewer (usually Lead)
3. Reviewer reviews within 2-4 hours
4. Address feedback, re-request review
5. Approval → Merge → Deploy

**Review Checklist**: See [QUALITY_STANDARDS.md](./QUALITY_STANDARDS.md)

## Communication Guidelines

### Slack Channels

- `#general` - Announcements, celebrations
- `#dev` - Engineering discussions
- `#design` - Design reviews and discussions
- `#marketing` - Marketing updates
- `#random` - Non-work chat
- `#bugs` - Bug reports and triage

### Response Time Expectations

| Urgency | Expected Response Time |
|---------|------------------------|
| **Critical** (production down) | < 30 minutes |
| **High** (blocking work) | < 2 hours |
| **Medium** (questions, reviews) | < 4 hours |
| **Low** (nice-to-have) | < 24 hours |

### Working Hours

**Core hours**: 10 AM - 4 PM PT (overlap for meetings)  
**Flexibility**: Async-friendly, results matter more than hours  
**Meetings**: Minimize, only when necessary

## Decision-Making Framework

### Types of Decisions

**Type 1 (Reversible)**: Made quickly by responsible person
- UI copy changes
- A/B test variations
- Feature flag toggles
- Social media posts

**Type 2 (Costly to Reverse)**: Discussed with team, CEO decides
- Architecture choices (TCA, Supabase)
- Major feature additions
- Pricing changes
- Brand changes

**Type 3 (Irreversible)**: Full team discussion, CEO final say
- Fundraising terms
- Team hiring/firing
- Major pivots
- Legal commitments

## Performance & Growth

### Performance Reviews

**Frequency**: Quarterly (every 3 months)

**Format**:
1. Self-assessment (what went well, what to improve)
2. Manager assessment (performance, growth areas)
3. 1:1 discussion (30-60 minutes)
4. Goals for next quarter

**Criteria**:
- Execution (delivering on commitments)
- Quality (code, design, content)
- Collaboration (helping teammates)
- Growth (learning and improving)
- Impact (business results)

## Culture & Values

### How We Work

1. **Ship Fast, Learn Fast**: Speed over perfection
2. **AI-Augmented**: 80% of code AI-generated, human-reviewed
3. **User-Obsessed**: Every decision starts with user value
4. **Transparent**: Share openly, over-communicate
5. **No Egos**: Best idea wins, not loudest voice

### What We Value

✅ **Ownership**: Take responsibility, see things through  
✅ **Impact**: Focus on what moves the needle  
✅ **Quality**: Do it right, not just done  
✅ **Learning**: Grow daily, share knowledge  
✅ **Fun**: Enjoy the journey, celebrate wins

❌ **Avoid**:
- Blame culture
- Feature factories (shipping without learning)
- Meetings that could be Slack messages
- Unnecessary process
- Politics

## Onboarding New Members

**Week 1**:
- Read all documentation (this folder)
- Set up environment (Xcode, Slack, etc.)
- Complete first contribution (bug fix, doc update)
- 1:1 with all team members

**Week 2-4**:
- Pick up first feature
- Pair program with teammate
- Attend all ceremonies (standups, planning)
- Integrate into team

**Buddy System**:
- Each new hire assigned a buddy
- Buddy helps with questions, context
- Check-in daily for first 2 weeks
