## The Opportunity

### The Problem

**150 million Americans** struggle daily with "What should I wear?"

- Average person spends **18 minutes** deciding on outfits daily = 110 hours/year wasted
- **$1.8 trillion** spent annually on clothing, yet 40% rarely worn
- Existing wardrobe apps are **digital filing cabinets** (not intelligent assistants)
- Weather changes, but closet remains static

**Pain Points**:
1. "I have nothing to wear" (surrounded by clothes)
2. Buying duplicates (forgot what you own)
3. Poor weather decisions (outfit not suitable)
4. Style paralysis (too many choices)
5. Wasted money (unworn clothes)

### The Solution

**FitChekk**: AI-powered wardrobe assistant that **actually suggests what to wear today**.

Not just a catalog app - a **daily decision engine** considering:
- ✅ Today's weather + forecast
- ✅ Your calendar (work, date, gym)
- ✅ Your style preferences
- ✅ What you wore recently
- ✅ Seasonal appropriateness

**Key Insight**: People don't want to **manage** their wardrobe. They want to **use** it better.

## Technology Advantage

### AI-First Architecture

**Not just "AI-powered" marketing** - fundamentally built on AI:

1. **Image Understanding** (Gemini 2.5 Pro)
   - Best vision model available
   - Understands style, color, formality, season
   - Cost: $0.0001 per image (cheap)

2. **Outfit Intelligence** (Claude Sonnet 4)
   - Superior reasoning ("why this works")
   - Considers weather, occasion, history
   - Prompt caching = 90% cost reduction

3. **AI Gateway** (Portkey)
   - Multi-model failover (never breaks)
   - A/B test models easily
   - Cost optimization built-in
   - $1,000+/month savings

### Cost Efficiency

**At 10K users**:
- Infrastructure: $250/month (Supabase)
- AI: $300/month (with caching)
- **Total**: $550/month (~$0.05 per user)

**Competitors** (estimated):
- Firebase: $500-1000/month
- Direct AI APIs: $1,500-2,000/month
- **We're 3x cheaper** at same scale

**Why This Matters**: Higher margins → faster growth → better product
